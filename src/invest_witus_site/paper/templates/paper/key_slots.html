Alpaca key slots

Each Alpaca paper account has its own keys. A slot is one pair on the server: ALPACA_PAPER_KEY_ID__NAME and ALPACA_PAPER_SECRET_KEY__NAME, or the pair with no suffix, which is DEFAULT. One underscore before the name (ALPACA_PAPER_KEY_ID_NAME) is read as the same slot. Each Invest account that trades on paper is linked through one slot, so each plan has an Alpaca account and cash of its own.
