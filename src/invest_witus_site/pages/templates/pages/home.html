Invest.WitUS is a Python app for people learning to invest with the Learn.WitUS and ElementaryMBA courses. You choose a method a course teaches, run it on a paper (simulated) account first, and the app checks what you did against that method's rules. Every rule links to the lesson that teaches it.

Join the waitlist Read the FAQ

How it works

  1. Pick a method you learned. The first is index investing with a fixed monthly contribution; 12-1 momentum comes next.
  2. Practice it on paper. Paper by default. No money moves unless you connect a live account yourself, later, behind a checklist.
  3. Review yourself against the rules. A monthly review shows where you left your plan and sends you back to the lesson. It predicts nothing.

The project began from freeCodeCamp's course "Python Algorithmic Trading with SnapTrade, Massive, Alpaca" (Carnes, 2026), which builds a momentum app in Django and invites learners to extend it.
