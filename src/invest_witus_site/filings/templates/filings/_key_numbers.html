How to read this. Each figure is what the company reported to the SEC for that period, in its 10-K (a year) or 10-Q (a quarter); hover or focus a figure to see the exact tag, form, filing day and dates. "Derived" means the app worked it out: free cash flow is operating cash flow less capital expenditure, and a quarter a filing reports only as "year to date" is that total less the one before it (the fourth quarter is the year less nine months). Shares, cash and debt are values on the period's last day. Compare a figure with the same period a year earlier; one number on its own says little. Lesson coming.
