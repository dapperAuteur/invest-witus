How to read this. The method acts on rank, not on the return itself: at each rebalance it holds whatever sits in the top {{ changes.top_n }}. An arrow up means the candidate's 12-1 return now ranks higher among your candidates than it did last month; "joined" or "left" means the move crossed the top {{ changes.top_n }} line, which is when the method buys or sells. Every month is ranked with your candidates as they are today, so a month before you changed the list shows how today's list would have ranked then. "not ranked" means a close is missing at one end of that month's window; the app never fills it in. Lesson coming.

Arithmetic on stored closes of the candidates you chose. A past rank is not a forecast. Information, not advice.
